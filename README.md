# iBeeSkilled Internship – Machine Learning & AI

## 📌 Internship Details
- **Organization:** Beeskilled
- **Domain:** Machine Learning & AI
- **Mode:** Online
- **Duration:** 6 Weeks
- **Start Date:** 31 July 2026
- **End Date:** 11 September 2026

## 📖 Weekly Workflow
For each week (Week 1 – Week 6):
1. Download the study material.
2. Complete the assignment.
3. Paste the answer in the submission box.
4. Click **Mark as Completed**.
5. The next week's content will unlock automatically.

## 📝 Quiz
- Unlocks after completing **Week 4**.
- Maximum **3 attempts**.
- Enter the correct **Name**, **Email**, and **Course** before submitting.

## 🎥 Final Submission
Upload a **Week 4 Assignment Explanation Video** with:
- Clear explanation
- Good audio quality
- Public Google Drive link

**Note:** Internship completion requires this submission.

## 🏆 Certificate Requirements
- Complete all 6 weeks.
- Submit the quiz.
- Upload the Week 4 explanation video.
- Pass the verification process.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes
- Incorrect username format.
- Private Google Drive link.
- Forgetting to click **Mark as Completed**.
- Selecting the wrong course in the quiz.
